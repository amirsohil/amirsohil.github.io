The Problem

0
GOALS SCORED
0
GAMES PLAYED

Pelé's scoring record of 1,284 goals across 1,375 games is a number so large that it's almost impossible to truly grasp. Stated on its own, it doesn't communicate the density or duration behind it.

The Approach

Rather than visualising totals, every one of the 1,284 recorded goals is rendered as its own cell in a large mosaic grid, meant to be read left to right, top to bottom, with each cell individually hoverable for its own detail. A restrained, high-contrast black-and-gold design keeps the piece reading as a tribute poster rather than a dashboard, without sacrificing the underlying granularity.

The Outcome

The mosaic does something the number alone can't. It makes the scale of the record visible rather than just stated. You're not told 1,284 goals happened; you see the shape of 1,284 individual cells, while still being able to drop into any single goal for its own detail.

A career total gets a headline number. The least we could do for a record built goal by goal over two decades is a grid worth spending time in.
