About

I make numbers become visuals, tools, worlds. Whatever the story needs.

I'm Amir, a data scientist and visual storyteller. I build models, pipelines, and analytical systems for a living, but the thing I am most well-known for is the way I turn data into narrative. My work has been recognised in global open-data competitions and shown at national exhibitions in Sri Lanka.

I graduated in Data Science from Coventry University as a Gold Medallist with a 4.0 GPA. Over the past four and a half years I've worked across the full data lifecycle, from mining, cleaning, statistical modelling, and machine learning to deployment, but my strength has always been the end-to-end product: taking raw data and shaping it into something polished, intuitive, and decision-ready.

Right now, I'm exploring new domains, working on more ambitious projects, and growing my freelance work while looking for opportunities to work with people and teams around the world.

Off the pitch

I'm a lifelong Liverpool supporter and have played competitively for both school and university teams, usually as a left-winger or centre-midfielder. Football taught me creativity, discipline, and, more than anything, how to see the whole field. That's more or less how I approach data.
