The Great Reordering

The Qur'an is read today in an order that has little to do with the sequence it was revealed in. This traces all 114 surahs from their place in the mushaf to their place in history — as reconstructed by two separate chronological traditions.

On this chart — Mushaf position is a surah's place in the standard printed Qur'an used today. Traditional chronology follows the sequence commonly cited across modern Qur'an editions and Islamic scholarship; Nöldeke chronology follows the 19th-century academic reconstruction proposed by German orientalist Theodor Nöldeke. Both orderings are scholarly reconstructions rather than settled fact, and other traditions exist.
